Features

Built to be checked,
not just believed.

Ranking that reads

Embeddings shortlist, then a model reads the READMEs. Keyword-stuffed empty repos don't win.

Verified quotes

Every snippet is matched against your repo text before display. Unverifiable lines are discarded.

Approval gates

Three checkpoints, and a paid step that only runs on the shortlist you approved.

Go back any time

Click a finished step to return to it. Your earlier choices are still ticked where you left them.

Cached, not re-read

Repos and their embeddings are stored. A second job description reuses everything but the new text.

Shareable results

Each run saves to its own link, with the job description attached. Past runs stay in your history.

Coming next

Resume bullets, written from the same evidence.

Approved picks become bullet points for the projects section — drafted only from what your repository actually says, then dropped into your existing LaTeX resume as a new version you review line by line.
