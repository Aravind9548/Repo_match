About

Built because
tailoring a resume
is weekly work.

Every posting wants a different half of what you've built. Picking which three projects to show, and writing why they matter, took an hour each time — and the answer was always already in the repositories.

RepoMatch does the reading and the ranking. The judgement stays with the person applying, because a model that never opened the pull request shouldn't get the last word on your work.

Principles
  • The costly step waits for a human.
  • Claims carry quotes, or they're cut.
  • Nothing is re-read that hasn't changed.
